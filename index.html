📝 A2P 10DLC Registration
Registers business as a legitimate SMS sender with carriers
Takes 2–4 weeks to approve
Required before ANY business SMS automation can run
Without it: SMS gets flagged as spam and blocked
With it: deliverability stays above 95%
Most roofing competitors skip this — they get filtered

Owner team does zero manual follow-up
Consistent regardless of how busy the crew is
